Original retention: 7 days
Originals and thumbnails are deleted only after 7 days AND all required backups have been verified. Failed uploads keep their local files. Older, unregistered originals are preserved.


Advanced: pause transfers

Pausing keeps accepting copies for selected destinations. Pending files use Pi disk space. In-flight transfers may finish.

 



Advanced: retire a destination for historical pending photos

Only completed scans with a verified backup at the other destination qualify. This cancels the missing backup requirement; it does not change future scans or delete cloud files. Eligible originals are still retained for 7 days.
